Questions

What is GMP?

Grey Market Premium is the unofficial premium an IPO trades at before it lists. A 20% GMP on a ₹100 issue suggests the market expects it to list around ₹120. It's a sentiment signal, not a guarantee — listings can go either way.

Is it really free?

Yes. One alert a day, no hidden plan. We'll only ever add optional extras later.

How do I stop the messages?

Reply STOP on WhatsApp, or use the unsubscribe page. You're removed right away.

Do you apply for IPOs on my behalf?

No. We never ask for your PAN, bank or demat details. You apply yourself through your broker or bank's UPI/ASBA.

What do you do with my number?

We use it only to send these alerts. We never sell or share it. Ask us to delete it any time.
